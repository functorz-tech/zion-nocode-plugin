# Check before replying

Look at a capture of each screen you built or restyled. Fix what fails, then reply.

## Every client

- [ ] One clear focal point per screen; the primary action is the only thing in the primary colour, apart from selected states.
- [ ] Every colour, radius, spacing, font and shadow references a theme variable; no one-off hex unless the user named it for that component.
- [ ] Body and secondary text contrast at least 4.5:1 against their background; dividers and input outlines visible.
- [ ] Related things sit closer to each other than to anything else; spacing follows the 4/8 rhythm of the theme's spacing presets.
- [ ] Three text levels that differ in weight, not only size; headings carry a line height.
- [ ] Cards lift with a shadow or a border, not both; no card inside a card.
- [ ] No gradient, glow or animation added only as decoration.
- [ ] None of the habits that read as generated unless the style asks for them: a small all-caps label above every heading, meta text joined with middle dots, an arrow appended to button text, one word in a heading coloured or bolded for interest, 01 / 02 / 03 markers on content that is not a sequence, every surface on the same radius and the same soft grey shadow.
- [ ] Icons from one family and one style (all outline or all filled at a level); no emoji standing in for icons.
- [ ] Selected tabs and options look selected; disabled controls look disabled.
- [ ] Empty, loading and error states read as designed, not as blank space.
- [ ] Form fields have visible labels, and errors appear next to the field.

## Mini-program and mobile

- [ ] Tap areas at least 44 × 44; nothing touches the screen edge; fixed bars clear the safe area and do not hide the last list item.
- [ ] No hover styles or pointer cursors.
- [ ] Every tappable thing looks tappable (fill, outline or chevron).

## Web

- [ ] Paragraphs stop at about 680 px; sections breathe (64 px and up on desktop).
- [ ] Buttons, clickable cards and rows have a hover state and a pointer cursor.
- [ ] The phone breakpoint was checked: rows wrap or stack, nothing overflows sideways.
